Privacy Policy for TIOP Smart Device Application

Last Updated: August 11, 2025
Effective Date: August 11, 2025

1. Data Collection

We collect the following data when you use our app with TIOP01/TIOP02 devices:

  • Device Data: Camera feeds, thermal imaging results, sensor readings (temperature/humidity)
  • Account Information: Email address for account creation
  • Usage Data: Feature usage frequency and error logs

2. Data Usage

Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Providing real-time monitoring functionality
  • Improving detection algorithms (e.g., cry detection, motion tracking)
  • Troubleshooting technical issues

3. Data Storage & Security

All sensitive data is protected with:

  • End-to-end AES-256 encryption during transmission
  • Google Cloud Platform storage with access controls
  • Automatic data deletion after 30 days of inactivity

4. Third-Party Services

We integrate with:

  • Google Firebase: For crash reporting and analytics
  • Amazon AWS: For video stream processing (TIOP02 only)

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR/CCPA you may:

  • Request data access at privacy@tiop-tech.com
  • Opt-out of data collection in app Settings
  • Delete your account and associated data
